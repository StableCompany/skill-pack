# Annual Planning Prep Guide

Two days to look back at <YEAR REVIEWED>, get honest about where we stand, and set the plan for <YEAR PLANNED>. This guide walks you through both days in the order they will run, and gives you space to do your thinking before you walk in the room.

**Four things these two days are designed to produce**

1. **An honest look back.** What we won, what we missed, and what got in the way.
2. **One clear picture.** Where we are headed, with everyone on the leadership team on the same page.
3. **A plan for <YEAR PLANNED>.** The numbers, the few goals that matter most, and the rocks for the first 90 days.
4. **The biggest issues worked.** The obstacles that could stall next year, named and solved together.

*A plan only counts if it outlives the session. Come rested, come prepared, and come ready to say what needs to be said in the room, so there is no meeting after the meeting.*

## Before you arrive

Arrive with the checklist done and the prep pages filled in, so the room can spend its time deciding.

### At least a week before the session

*Note: Open <EOS PLATFORM> to review the V/TO, this year's rocks, the scorecard and the issues list.*

- ☐ **Look back at your year.** Your rocks by quarter, your numbers, your wins and misses.
- ☐ **Read the V/TO.** Mark anything that no longer feels true or clear.
- ☐ **Look outside.** Fill in the SWOT and trends pages, with one source you trust.
- ☐ **Predict next year.** Your forecast for revenue, profit and the numbers you own.
- ☐ **Fill in this guide.** The prep pages are yours. Handwritten is fine.

### On the days

- ☐ **Bring your calendar and your <YEAR PLANNED> time off.** We will set dates for the year.
- ☐ **Plan for a working lunch both days.** <FOOD OWNER> will arrange the food.
- ☐ **Clear both days.** Phones down, laptops closed unless you are presenting data.

### The two days at a glance

**Day one: look back and look out**

| Time | Segment | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 30 min | Check-In | Best news of the year, what is working and what is not |
| 1.5 hours | The Year in Review | Numbers, rocks by quarter, wins, misses |
| 1.5 hours | SWOT | Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats |
| 1 hour | Trends | What is changing outside, and what it means for us |
| 2 hours | V/TO Review | The long-range picture, to 100% on the same page |
| 30 min | Issues List and Wrap | Every new issue on the board; what we carry into day two |

**Day two: set the plan**

| Time | Segment | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 15 min | Check-In | What changed overnight, expectations for today |
| 2 hours | The 1-Year Plan | Revenue, profit, measurables, the few goals for the year |
| 2 hours | First-Quarter Rocks | Company rocks for the first 90 days, then each leader's own |
| 2 hr 15 min | IDS | Identify, Discuss, Solve the issues that could slow the year |
| 30 min | Next Steps and Conclude | To-dos, what we tell the company, rate the two days |

*Times are approximate. IDS flexes to fit the issues in the room.*

## Prep page 3 of 7 · 1.5 hours: SWOT

> **In the room**

> Strengths and weaknesses are inside us: our people, process, product and money. Opportunities and threats are outside: customers, competitors, the market.

## Prep page 4 of 7 · 1 hour: Trends

> **In the room**
> We look up from the business at what is changing around it. Each of us brings at least one trend, and one source we trust for it: an industry association, a study, a customer, a supplier.
> For each trend the question is the same: if this keeps going, what does it mean for us next year?
> Watch for the assumption that a good year will simply come back. A trends check is how a team catches it in time.

## Prep page 5 of 7 · 2 hours: V/TO Review
