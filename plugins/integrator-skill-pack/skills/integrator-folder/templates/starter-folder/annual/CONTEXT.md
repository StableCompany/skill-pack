# Annual planning
One folder per year being planned: `YYYY`. Prepping in November 2026 for 2027 means `annual/2027/`.
- `leader-prep-guide.md` goes to the leadership team before annual planning.
- `integrator-year-review.md` is my own look back at the year: priorities done by quarter, the numbers, and the issues that kept coming back. It stays with me unless I share it.
- `year-calendar.md` is next year at a glance: every meeting in our rhythm, the days we are closed, PTO, big events, and the working days in each quarter.
- **Written by:** annual-planning-prep. **Read by:** annual-planning-prep, and rock-writer for closed days and working days.
- **Every number and every status names where it came from:** a file, our tool with the date it was read, or "you said" with the date.
- **After the session,** say "cascade this" to share the plan, and "update my priorities" so integrator-folder updates `company.md`.
