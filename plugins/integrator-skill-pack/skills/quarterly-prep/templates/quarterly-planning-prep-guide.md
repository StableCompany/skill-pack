# Quarterly Planning Prep Guide

A full day to look back, get on the same page, and set the 90-day plan. This guide walks you through the session in the order it will run, and gives you space to prepare your thinking before you walk in the room.

**Three things this day is designed to produce**

1. **A clear vision.** Everyone on the leadership team on the same page.
2. **A clear plan.** The 3 to 7 things that must get done in the next 90 days.
3. **Key issues resolved.** The obstacles that could slow the quarter, tackled together.

*The quarterly is about execution. Come rested, come honest, and come prepared to say what needs to be said. The most useful sessions are the ones where the team is open with each other and just puts it on the table.*

## Before you arrive

Preparation is most of the work. If every leader arrives with the checklist below done and the prep pages filled in, the room can spend its time on decisions instead of catch-up.

### At least 48 hours before the session

*Note: Access <EOS PLATFORM> to review the V/TO, rocks, scorecard and other materials.*

- ☐ **Review the V/TO.** Read every section. Note anything that no longer feels true or clear.
- ☐ **Predict the next 90 days.** Come with your best forecast for revenue, profit, and the key measurables on the Scorecard.
- ☐ **Draft what you want to accomplish.** Rough Rock ideas are enough.
- ☐ **Fill in this guide.** The prep pages that follow are yours. Handwritten is fine.

### On the day

- ☐ **Bring your calendar.** You will be setting due dates and the date of the next quarterly.
- ☐ **Plan for a working lunch around noon.** <FOOD OWNER> will arrange the food.
- ☐ **Clear the day.** Phones down, laptops closed unless you are presenting data.

### The day at a glance

| Time | Segment | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 15 min | Check-In | Positive reports, surface issues, set expectations |
| 30 min | Review Prior Quarter | Numbers first, then Rocks. Completion percentage and lessons learned |
| 1 hour | V/TO Review | Walk the whole vision, section by section. Confirm 100% on the same page |
| <EOS TOOLS ROW, OR DELETE> | | |
| 2 hours | Rocks | Set the 3 to 7 company Rocks and each leader's individual Rocks for the next 90 days |
| <IDS LENGTH> | IDS | Identify, Discuss, Solve the issues that could impede the quarter |
| 7 min | Next Steps | Recap to-dos, update tools, confirm the cascade to the company |
| 8 min | Conclude | Feedback, were expectations met, rate the day 1 to 10 |

*Times are approximate. Rocks and IDS flex to fit the issues in the room.*

## Prep page 3 of 6 · 1 hour: V/TO Review

> **In the room**
> We walk the entire Vision/Traction Organizer one section at a time. The standard is 100% on the same page, all rowing in the same direction.
> For each section the question is the same: is this still true, is it clear, and are we living it? Challenge everything.
> Any people issues get named and go on the Issues List. Right Person issues are about core values. Right Seat issues are about GWC: get it, want it, capacity to do it.
> Anything incomplete or off goes on the Issues List. We decide later whether today is the day to nail it down.

Read your V/TO before the session and mark each section below. Bring specifics for anything you mark as needing discussion.

| Section | The question | Same page | Needs discussion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Core Values** | Who we are. Are we hiring, firing, reviewing, and rewarding based on these? | ☐ | ☐ |
| **Core Focus** | Why we exist and what we are great at. Are all our people, processes, and systems built to drive it? | ☐ | ☐ |
| **10-Year Target** | The long-term goal we are all going toward. Still the right destination? | ☐ | ☐ |
| **Marketing Strategy** | Target market, three uniques, proven process, guarantee. Are sales and <CORE FUNCTION> laser-focused on the right <TARGET CUSTOMER TYPE>? | ☐ | ☐ |
| **3-Year Picture** | The numbers and the picture. Do we all see it, want it, and believe it? | ☐ | ☐ |

## Prep page 4 of 6: Issues List

> **In the room**
> Everyone takes five quiet minutes to list new issues. Then we go around the table and get every issue on the board. Last call before we move on.
> No solving and no discussion while the list is built. That discipline is what makes the afternoon work.
> We also review the V/TO Issues List from last quarter. Each item gets one of three answers: kill it, today, or not today.

## Prep page 5 of 6 · 2 hours: Rock Brainstorm

> **In the room**
> Company Rocks first. Looking at the 1-Year Plan, last quarter's Rocks, and the Issues List: what are the 3 to 7 most important things that MUST get done this quarter?
> Every proposed Rock goes on the board. Then we keep, kill, or combine down to the top 3 to 7, write each one SMART, and assign one owner.
> We pick the due date, which is also the date of the next quarterly, and set revenue, profit, and measurable goals for the 90 days.
> Then individual Rocks. Each leader shares their 3 to 7 priorities and the team confirms they are achievable. The standard for the quarter is 80% completion or better.

## Prep page 6 of 6 · <IDS LENGTH>: IDS, Tackling Key Issues

> **In the room**
> We follow the pure IDS process: Identify the real issue, Discuss it once without going in circles, Solve it with a decision and a to-do.
> The team picks the top three issues first, then solves as many as time permits. Every conclusion that is an action lands on the <WEEKLY MEETING NAME> To-Do List with an owner.
> What does not get solved either stays on the V/TO Issues List for next quarter or moves to the weekly <WEEKLY MEETING NAME> Issues List.
