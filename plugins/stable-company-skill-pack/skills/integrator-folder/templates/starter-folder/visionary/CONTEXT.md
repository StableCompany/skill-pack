# My Visionary
Everything between me and my Visionary.
- `open-items.md`: what we owe each other. Headings: I owe <Visionary> · <Visionary> owes me · To raise next time · Ideas parked · Closed. Each line: the item, the date asked, the due date or `date?`, and the file it came from. The exact line formats are at the top of the file.
- `YYYY-MM-DD-same-page.md`: the agenda for each same-page meeting, with "What came out of it" added after.
- **Written by:** same-page-prep. clarity-break adds to "To raise next time", after my yes.
- **Read by:** same-page-prep, clarity-break.
- **Describe my Visionary's ideas and asks fairly.** Opinions about them as a person stay off the page.
